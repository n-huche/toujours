# Account

## Identity

- An account is identified by its e-mail address.
- There are no passwords. To sign in, the person enters their e-mail and receives a one-time verification code.
- An account is created implicitly the first time an e-mail address is verified.
- The browser stays signed in until the owner signs out.

## Ownership

- An account owns at most one page. The page is not shared or co-edited.
- An account has at most one subscription at a time.

## Deletion

- The owner can delete their account at any time from `/conta`.
- Deleting an account ends its subscription, permanently deletes the page immediately, and releases its slug. There is no recovery window. Unused prepaid time is not refunded except through the 7-day refund.

## Locale

- The product is offered in Brazilian Portuguese, priced in BRL, and all dates use the `America/Sao_Paulo` time zone.
