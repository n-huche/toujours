# Subscription and billing

## Plans

| Offer | Images | Characters | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trial (no subscription) | 50 | 500,000 | Free for 48 h, once per account |
| Paid | 150 | 1,500,000 | R$ 5 / month |

There is one paid plan. It is prepaid: charged on the day the subscription starts and then monthly on the same day of the month (on the last day for shorter months). An account has at most one page; the plan does not sell extra pages.

## General rules

- An account has at most one subscription.
- Payment is by credit card only.

## Limits and usage

- **Images and characters:** measured on the page's working copy. The editor prevents exceeding them; publishing is refused while the page is above a limit.
- The owner can always see how many images and characters are used.

## Renewal and payment failure

- If a charge fails, it is retried once a day, **twice more** (three attempts in total, including the first). The page stays online meanwhile.
- If the charge still fails, the subscription ends and the page becomes Expired.
- An account may subscribe again later; any unpaid **charge** must be settled first. The expired page can then be restored within its recovery window.

## Cancellation

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. The page stays online until then, and the owner may undo the cancellation before that. Afterwards, the page becomes Expired.

## Refunds

- Within 7 days of starting a subscription, the owner may cancel and receive a full refund of any amount charged (Brazilian consumer law, CDC art. 49). The page becomes Expired.
- There are no other refunds.
