# Page Manager

## Subscription options

- **Free:** 1 page, up to 50 images, and 500,000 characters, for 48 hours at no cost.
- **Essential:** 1 page, up to 150 images, and 1,500,000 characters per page, for R$3/month.
- **Trio:** up to 3 pages, up to 150 images, and 1,500,000 characters per page, for R$5/month.
- **Studio:** up to 10 pages, up to 300 images, and 3,000,000 characters per page, for R$10/month.
- **Flexible:** no fixed limit on pages or content. The user is charged based on resource usage over the previous month: R$1 per page/month, R$0.20 per 100 images/month, and R$0.02 per million characters/month.

## Rules

Any user can create their first page for free and keep it active for 48 hours.

After the 48-hour period, the user can start a subscription to keep the page active continuously for as long as the subscription remains valid.

If the user does not start a subscription, the page will remain available for recovery for 7 days. After this period, its content will be permanently deleted.

Users can freely create, edit, and delete pages, subject to the page, image, and character limits defined by their subscription.

Users can track the number of images and characters used on each page, as well as the total number of images and characters used across all their pages.

On the Flexible plan, users can also track how their usage of pages, images, and characters affects their billing.
